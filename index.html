How to use this tracker

There is no set order. Use any of these on their own or together to find the bills of interest to you.

  • Choose a chamber - Choose All, House or Senate below. Every number, chart and list on the page updates to match.
  • Filter with a chart - Click or tap any bar in the charts, or a slice of the local government ring, to list those bills under Find a bill. One chart filter applies at a time.
  • Search - Type a bill number, author or keyword in the Find a bill search box. It also works on top of a chamber or chart filter.
  • Clear a chart filter - Select the same bar again, or the × on the filter tag above the list, to see every bill again.

Use this tracker as a resource, not the final word

This tracker is a research tool, not an official record or legal advice. Bill information comes from Texas Legislature Online. The summaries and the local government flags are prepared with the help of AI and are judgment calls that can be incomplete or wrong, and a bill can change after this page was last updated.

Before you act on a bill, or publish or share information from this page, check the official bill text and status on Texas Legislature Online and consult your attorney or government relations staff.

Source: Texas Legislature Online (TLO) bill history and bill text, Texas Legislative Council (ftp.legis.state.tx.us/bills/90R). Counts include House and Senate bills and joint resolutions, not simple or concurrent resolutions. Topics are Legislative Reference Library subject areas. "Affects local government" and the government types are a reviewer judgment based on each bill's introduced text. A bill counts when it directly changes the powers, duties, finances, governance or officials of a local government; indirect effects are not counted. Once a Legislative Budget Board fiscal note is posted, bills marked as not affecting local government are checked against its Local Government Impact section. Updated each evening during the session.
